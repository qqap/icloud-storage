# Apple Gift Card Trick for iCloud

## The Problem with Recurring Cards

- iCloud is month-to-month only — no annual prepay
- If your card expires or fails, plan lapses and data is at risk
- Apple retries silently; you may not notice until damage is done

## The Fix: Apple Account Balance

Apple lets you load credit onto your Apple Account. All subscriptions (iCloud, Apple TV+, etc.) draw from that balance first — before any card is charged.

**Result:** load $12–15 once a year → iCloud pays itself for 12–15 months, no card needed.

## How to Do It

1. Buy an Apple Gift Card — digital or physical
   - Apple Store: [apple.com/shop/gift-cards](https://www.apple.com/shop/gift-cards)
   - Amazon (sometimes on sale)
   - Any grocery store / pharmacy
2. On iPhone: **Settings → [your name] → Add Funds to Apple Account**
3. Tap **Redeem Gift Card or Code**
4. Camera scans the code, balance loads instantly
5. Done — iCloud charges that balance monthly

## Math for 50GB Plan ($1/mo)

| Gift card | Months covered |
|-----------|---------------|
| $12 | 12 months |
| $15 | 15 months |
| $25 | 25 months |

Buy a $15 card once a year and forget about it.

## Notes

- Balance never expires (Apple gift cards have no expiry in the US)
- Works for all Apple subscriptions, not just iCloud
- You can stack multiple gift cards — balance just adds up
